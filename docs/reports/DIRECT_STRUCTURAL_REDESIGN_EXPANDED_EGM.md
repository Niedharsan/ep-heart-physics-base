## EGM changes

The interval trainer now contains 11 synthetic channels:

1. II
2. V1
3. HRA
4. His d
5. His p
6. RVA
7. CS 9-10
8. CS 7-8
9. CS 5-6
10. CS 3-4
11. CS 1-2

The coronary-sinus channels have separate deterministic activation times and separate waveform arrays.

Task 5 para-Hisian pacing uses the same complete channel layout and displays an unchanged distal-to-proximal retrograde CS sequence across the paired pacing states.

## Boundary

The signals remain deterministic schematic teaching signals. They are not patient recordings and are not clinically validated.
